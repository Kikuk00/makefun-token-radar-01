How the implied value is worked out

The peer ratio for a category is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of that category's live-token protocols (at least 3 peers); a thin category falls back to the global median. Growth is the protocol's own 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%. Maturity is its age since DefiLlama began tracking it, capped at 3 years. Breadth is +3% per extra chain, at most +15%. No name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row, and the board covers the whole watchlist plus every other rankable pre-token protocol the snapshot holds.
