How the implied value is worked out

The ratio is fitted, not assumed: on the 128 live-token protocols that carry both a market cap and a TVL, least squares gives log(mcap/TVL) = a + b·log(TVL), with a negative slope — the bigger a protocol's TVL, the lower the multiple the market pays for it. Growth is the protocol's own 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120% at half weight; maturity is its age since DefiLlama began tracking it, rising from 0.6 to 1.0 over 3 years; breadth adds +3% per extra chain, at most +15%. Growth, maturity and breadth are stated priors, not fitted coefficients. No name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row, and the board covers all 758 snapshot protocols — the whole watchlist plus every other protocol it holds.
